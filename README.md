## Make the view your own

**Compare.** Place two models side by side, each with its own view, cutaway, labels and disassembly controls. Comparison notes explain the specimens represented; the views are fitted independently, without a common physical scale.

**Create.** Compose images and short films in Studio. Choose landscape, portrait or square; export a PNG with an optional transparent background, or record an orbit, disassembly or biological process.

**Return and share.** Pick up where you left off from the homepage. Share a link to a model, a process in progress or both comparison panes, with the view and display settings included.

<summary>Export and sharing details</summary>

Images support 1920 or 2560 pixel long edges. Videos use MP4 or WebM according to browser support. Titles and labels are optional; exports retain the BioScape project mark and teaching context, without an author-name watermark.

Shared links use the current site address. A link made on a local server requires access to that server.

## About the models

BioScape is made for exploring and teaching biology. Each model has its own scope, explanations and references. Geometry, proportions and timing include teaching simplifications; experimental structures are attributed separately.

