All cutaway sheets show the same point; every whole-shell case omits that caption. This visually supports the prior exact-object and default-camera first-hit ray regression. All other molecular/product anchors remain associated with extant objects.
- **R03, Cas9 identities:** HNH and RuvC keep their own domains through default, no-PAM and mismatch branches. RNA and DNA captions stay on their own rails; rejected/failed branches do not retain a displaced-strand caption. The PAM cubes can still overlap the protein pocket in projection, and the small positive-branch cut gaps remain difficult to read beside the domains; molecular identity and exact cut geometry are supported by the earlier independent tests, not established anew by an unobscured atomic view.
- **R04, SOS anchors/absence:** wildtype transcript captions reach visible response RNA; before transcription they remain absent. Noncleavable LexA remains an intact dimer and no polymerase/transcript/autocleavage caption is introduced. The two DNA direction labels follow their own strand endpoints during changes in helical phase.
- **R05, shared opaque-box avoidance:** the final English RNA p=.575 original exposes both CCR4–NOT lobes, with its caption above/right; the final SOS start and p=.795 originals keep the gap caption below the upper DNA and RecA above it. These corroborate the [shared repair and three-pose Chinese pixel/rendered signoff](player-label-layout.md).
