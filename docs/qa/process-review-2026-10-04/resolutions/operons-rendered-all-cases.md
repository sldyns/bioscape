- **Lac:** the earlier off-target labels now reach the visible LacI/CAP, DNA site or growing transcript. Inducer-bound/unbound states and transcript-number contrast survive condition changes. No sampled RNA bridge disappearance was observed.
- **Trp:** RNA region/hairpin leaders target the drawn backbone or named protein; the growing endpoint follows the visible 3′ tip. Both antitermination and termination are now rendered-condition evidence. At p=.885 in the terminating branch RNAP is still visible and the text says `Attenuator 3′ end`; at p=1 RNAP is absent and the text says `Leader RNA released`.
- **GAL:** the initial ligand-free Gal3 text and later ligand-bound wording agree with the visible model. Gal80, Gal3, the coactivator, repression complex, DNA and RNA endpoints remain tied to their represented targets. `Nuclear detail` remains an intentional region annotation.
- **HOG:** relay/kinase/channel labels reach their respective objects, rather than neighboring glycerol. Shrinking-cell glycerol remains inside the membrane in both active and inhibited cases, and nucleus/whole-cell response annotations remain regional.

## Limits and handoff

- These 112 still frames establish sampled rendered-condition coverage only. They cannot independently prove smooth interpolation, causal timing between all samples, every transition boundary, sustained playback performance, or absence of every inter-frame artifact. Earlier source/geometry/mutation tests remain separate evidence in [`operons-rendered.md`](operons-rendered.md) and the owned logs.
- The capture batch may predate the root integrator’s shared two-pass leader/box rendering fix. Existing leader lines crossing earlier-drawn label boxes were explicitly excluded from new module issue registration; no molecular anchor was moved to mask that known shared problem. This batch does not by itself visually accept the later shared draw-order fix.
- The evidence is limited to the provided 960×640, fixed-view capture for the supported roots and these 16 controls. This review does not claim mobile/device, camera-orbit, export, hosted-release or deployment acceptance.
- No new issue was confirmed, no additional product edit is requested by this review, and source remains frozen for integration. The report adds final sampled all-case visual evidence; it does not replace or rewrite the immutable Phase A audit.
