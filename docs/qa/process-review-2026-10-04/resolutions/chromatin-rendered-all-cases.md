## R-CH-02 — leaders cross other English label boxes (resolved shared P2)

This was reported to root before writing this report and is tracked as **`20261004-shared-capture-leader-layer-01`**. The historical observations below describe the `conditions-final-a-` defect; its fresh B resolution is recorded immediately afterward. **Plant genome, targeting removed:** in the [p=.585 original](../evidence/browser/conditions-final-a-037-plantGenome-plant-stage-4.webp) and [end original](../evidence/browser/conditions-final-a-037-plantGenome-plant-end.webp), the TOC/TIC leader runs diagonally from the pore near `(583, 177)` toward its lower-right label. The precursor leaders themselves reach the correct green/tan chains, and the chains visibly remain outside the envelopes. The problem is that an unrelated line runs through each explanatory text box.

**RdDM, early English frames:** in the [inactive p=.22 original](../evidence/browser/conditions-final-a-039-plantRdDM-plant-critical-220.webp), the Pol IV leader runs approximately from `(162, 27)` to `(205, 101)` and intersects the right edge of the RDR2 text box around `x178–190, y54–72`. The active/inactive sheets show the same top-left label arrangement at the early critical samples. This is the same leader-box avoidance class; enzyme targets remain correct. The earlier [critical-frame report](chromatin-rendered-critical.md) correctly describes molecular states but did not identify this small English label-box crossing. This report adds that narrower layout finding without withdrawing its molecular-state observations.

No label-box-to-label-box overlap, principal-geometry clipping or new remote-synthesis/incorrect-compartment defect was observed in this batch. Long leaders passing through otherwise unlabeled scene space are not classified as failures; the issue above is specifically a line crossing another label's text box.
