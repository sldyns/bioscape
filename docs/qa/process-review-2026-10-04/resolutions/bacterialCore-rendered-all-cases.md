## Scope and remaining limits

- This was a read-only review of the supplied files. No browser was operated and no product source, issue ledger or existing audit was changed.
- All registered cases for these four IDs in the supplied final index were covered. This does not extend to unregistered roots, extra camera angles, other viewport sizes, or every language.
- These are stage samples and native irregular seek captures. They cannot establish smoothness, lack of transient popping between samples, full continuous playback, input responsiveness or frame-rate behavior. The previously retained numeric/science regressions remain separate evidence.
- The shared label painter's later leader lines crossing previously painted text boxes were already reported and centrally changed to a two-pass draw order. This capture set may predate that change; the division blocked final frame and transformation right-side label stack show the known overlap class. It is not registered as a new bacterialCore finding, and true geometry anchors were not moved to conceal it. Fresh post-change integration capture remains the evidence required to accept the shared layout repair.
- DNA strand handedness, subpixel ComEC continuity and the transformation pairing transition were previously verified with geometry/source evidence. These stills do not independently replace those measurements; the native final heteroduplex's two strands are close together and are less distinguishable in the contact sheet than at original resolution.

The seven existing bacterialCore resolutions remain unchanged. This review adds **0 confirmed process-specific findings**.
