## Dynein and energy branches

The active dynein originals at p=.495 and p=.715 resolve the repaired small globular binding domains at the ends of the long stalks. One tip is visibly supported at the tubulin surface while its partner is raised in the sampled pose; the identity of the raised partner changes between these samples. The p=1 frame places both tips on the track near the minus-end side. The ATP-depleted p=1 original has both tips at the starting plus-side region, with the cargo already docked. These are positive rendered checks of the previously missing domain/contact geometry (`20261004-rna-01`).

The seven stills demonstrate net direction and branch behavior. They do not independently prove every alternating/irregular step, the entire backward-step event, force/kinetics, or uninterrupted contact throughout full playback. Those claims retain their separate geometry/time-sampling regression evidence.

## Nuclear import and receptor recycling

In all three exposed-NLS root sheets, cargo/alpha are nuclear at the late stages, the alpha label explicitly says retained in nucleus, and beta returns cytosolward with Ran. Ran is GTP-labelled before the terminal GDP-labelled image. All masked-NLS root sheets keep cargo and both receptors cytosolic, keep Ran-GTP nuclear, and do not show the retained-alpha wording. This depicts the beta/Ran return arm; the separate alpha/CAS recycling pathway is outside the model.

The p=.765 native image showed beta's old physical anchor hidden behind foreground green Ran. This is the separate P2 `20261004-rna-rendered-03`, approved and fixed using one stable point on an existing upper-front beta helix triangle. The model geometry, relative molecule positions, Ran anchor and camera are unchanged. The original p=.935 image was also opened: the exact fitted default-camera ray already resolves that old anchor to beta, correcting an earlier unfitted-camera probe. It is retained as a preservation check, not a second negative-control failure.

Local regression now passes 156 fitted-default-camera first-hit identity assertions across three roots, both NLS branches and 13 sampled poses, including .765/.935. The old .765 anchor hits Ran in all three roots; the repaired point hits beta. Each root/condition keeps one beta-local point through 1,005 seeks. The separate rendered gate is now also complete for the sampled poses: all six batch-E sheets were opened, and the exposed .765/.935 originals were inspected separately for cell, plant and yeast. Beta terminates on the upper purple helix, Ran-GTP terminates on the Ran complex, and the retained-alpha leader identifies the separate gold alpha structure. All three masked sheets preserve the correct cytosolic branch. No new issue was confirmed.

## Limits

- The accepted snapshot set uses English 960×640 exports for RNA processing, motor transport and organelle import, and Chinese 960×640 exports for the six fresh NPC cases. The new NPC Chinese labels were visually reviewed; post-fix NPC English layout was not recaptured in this supplement. This review does not establish UI controls, continuous per-case playback, frame-time, alternate camera, mobile/physical-device or publication acceptance.
- RNA sample progress: 0 / .145 / .305 / .475 / .705 / .915 / 1. NPC: 0 / .175 / .355 / .575 / .765 / .935 / 1. Motor: 0 / .165 / .315 / .495 / .715 / .915 / 1. Organelle: 0 / .175 / .355 / .565 / .775 / .925 / 1. Fine reaction boundaries and the exact central pore crossing are not all represented by these seven-frame sets.
- Real nuclear membrane/pore structure can occlude a receptor during central traversal. The authorized correction addresses the confirmed beta-to-Ran identity ambiguity in the default binding/recycling view; it does not force arbitrary-time/angle visibility through opaque structure.
- The shared leader-over-earlier-box drawing-order defect is handled by root's two-pass rendering repair. It is not duplicated as an RNA issue and no molecular anchor was moved to work around that shared box issue.
- No additional scientific or branch-state defect was confirmed in the eight non-NPC cases or six corrected NPC recaptures. Original Phase A records remain unchanged.
