# BioScape v1.1.6

发布日期为 2026 年 11 月 9 日。

[在线体验](https://sldyns.github.io/bioscape/) · [更新日志](../../CHANGELOG.md)

影像工作台加载失败时，错误提示留在工作台。关闭后可以在原来的结构、过程或对比场景继续探索，键盘焦点回到入口按钮。选择重新加载页面时，会先保存当前进度和视角。

验收覆盖工作台脚本请求失败、加载中取消、结构对比、中英文界面、390 像素窄视口、键盘焦点和页面重载。恢复后的真实工作台完成了取消和重试，下载的 1920×1080 视频为 15 秒，包含 450 帧时间戳，PNG 也成功导出。

## English

If Studio fails to load, the error stays in Studio. Closing it returns you to the same structure, process, or comparison and restores keyboard focus to the Studio button. Reloading saves the current view and progress first.

Validation covered an interrupted Studio request, cancellation while loading, comparison, both languages, a 390-pixel viewport, keyboard focus, and reload recovery. The recovered Studio completed a full-process 15-second 1920×1080 video with 450 timed frames, followed by a PNG download.
