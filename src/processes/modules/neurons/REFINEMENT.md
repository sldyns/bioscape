# Neuron / motor process refinement

Reference inspected: `src/processes/transcriptionProcess.js`, `src/scene/membraneDetails.js`, and `src/scene/cytoskeletonDetails.js`. Geometry is explanatory, not an atomic-coordinate reconstruction. Original organisms, stages, controls and source scope are retained.

- **actionPotential:** Previously two capsule channel halves and solid membrane rails. Now paired phospholipid headgroup leaflets and paired hydrophobic tails form a cutaway with actual channel holes; twelve four-domain pores contain repeated helical membrane bundles, extracellular collars and a gated central lumen. Sodium-channel domains are schematic domains of a pore-forming chain, not four independent Na-channel subunits. Six sequential membrane regions give the channels more screen space while preserving local Na/K flux and propagation. Static lipid geometry is instanced.
- **synapse:** Previously transparent sphere/lathe vesicle and capsule receptor pairs. Now opaque outer/inner cutaway vesicle surfaces expose the lumen, cut edges, fusion neck, and zipper-like SNARE bundles with calcium-sensor lobes. Bilayers contain actual circular pore clearances and double tails. Four-domain Ca pore, tetrameric AMPA transmembrane helix bundles and paired ligand-binding lobes, membrane-underlying receptor scaffold, and tripartite uptake-protein silhouette distinguish the complexes. The release trajectory stays inside the fusion neck; transmitter binds on the extracellular side and then clears through the lateral uptake site. Membrane fusion still switches between two topologies at the fusion stage rather than resolving a continuous molecular fusion simulation.
- **muscle:** Previously central rods, bead-chain actin and single ellipsoidal motor heads. Now each thin filament is a staggered two-start helix of clefted actin subunits, built with instancing; two tropomyosin strands follow the grooves and move by rotation. Troponin has a paired TnC calcium pocket, inhibitory arm and filament anchor. Myosin has a coiled-coil thick-filament surface, lever-arm light-chain collars and a split motor-domain cleft. Z-disc lattice and fixed-length filament sliding remain distinct. Heads remain synchronized as explicitly described in the stages.
- **ciliaryMotion:** Previously smooth closed cylinder doublets and detached motor beads. Now longitudinal A microtubules have outer/inner walls and open lumina; B microtubules have incomplete walls joined toward neighboring A walls. Enlarged transverse doublets show 13 A-wall tracks and an incomplete B wall assembled from repeated subunits. Longitudinal tubulin motifs are instanced and follow the absolute arclength-preserving bend. Dynein motors have collars, A-wall anchors and stalk connections to adjacent doublets; gold interdoublet links are retained. The cross-section is enlarged, and molecular spacing is illustrative.
