# Plant water processes — structural refinement

Reference inspected: `src/processes/transcriptionProcess.js`, `src/scene/chloroplastDetails.js`, and `src/scene/membraneDetails.js`. Existing biological scope, sources, stages, controls and process IDs are retained. Surface detail is schematic and enlarged for legibility, not an atomic or ultrastructural reconstruction.

- **Plasmolysis:** plain extruded wall and single membrane lines → fixed wall with three cellulose bundle layers and crossing strands; paired phospholipid headgroups, hydrophobic interior and continuous leaflet rails on the plasma membrane; separately modeled bilayer tonoplast and vacuolar cut lip. Membrane/vacuole details move with the original protoplast, while wall microfibrils remain stationary. Water and bath solutes remain visible above the section.
- **Stomata:** smooth translucent kidneys and isolated internal ellipsoids → bounded front cutaways, thick pore-facing wall, paired membrane cut edges and radial wall bundles. All wall/membrane/radial structures deform with the same guard-cell equations during opening and closure. Small chloroplasts now contain envelope/granum structure. The cutaways expose the vacuoles without making the whole cell uniformly transparent.
- **Xylem transport:** partially transparent smooth tube with separate rings → opaque, thickness-bearing cutaway vessel wall with geometric lateral pit apertures backed by thin primary-wall pit membranes; continuous helical secondary thickening; open simple perforation-plate rims at vessel-element junctions. The liquid column remains continuous. Leaf chloroplasts have internal grana rather than plain ovals. The model remains a compressed tissue section, not one giant cell.
- **Chloroplast movement:** green flattened ellipsoids with three surface marks → open double-envelope shells with distinct cut rims, three stacks of five thylakoid disks and connecting stroma lamellae. Organelles move and rotate as complete assemblies along the existing cortical route; the vacuole is not traversed. Genotype/illumination behavior remains unchanged.

`structuralDetail.js` shares shell, disk, rim and primitive buffers within each scene. Lipid headgroups/tails, grana, lateral pit membranes and dynamic radial wall bundles use instancing where appropriate. No geometry, material or scene nodes are created in update. There are no material swaps requiring an extra inventory.
