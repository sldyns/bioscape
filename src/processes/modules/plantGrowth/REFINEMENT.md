# Plant-growth process refinement

Inspected the accepted transcription process and `src/scene/membraneDetails.js` before editing. Changes are schematic mesoscale anatomy, not atomic reconstructions. Existing mechanisms, bilingual stages, sources, and condition controls remain. No thumbnails or shared renderer files changed.

- **plantDivision:** plain chromatids became condensed chromatin coils with packing domains and constricted centromere features. Solid phragmoplast rods became hollow protofilament cylinders assembled from instanced tubulin subunits. Solid vesicle beads became cutaway membrane carriers with visible lumen/cargo. The initial cell plate now has a tubulovesicular network before its paired membranes flatten into a partition. Parent wall cut edges expose lamellae. This preserves chromosome separation and outward plate growth.
- **cellWallGrowth:** six simple CESA blobs became six-lobed rosettes with grouped catalytic lobes, membrane-spanning helices, and convergent extracellular glucan outlets. Old wall fibers now contain twisted parallel strand bundles and matrix tethers. Newly deposited fibers have multi-strand structure. The membrane patch has paired lipid head rows and tails along exposed cut edges. Yielding versus restrained conditions still change expansion independently of deposition.
- **doubleFertilization:** two smooth enclosing hoops became two individually partitioned maternal integument cell layers with exposed depth and nuclei. Synergids gained filiform-apparatus wall folds. The pollen tube now exposes its wall and inner membrane/lumen through an open-front cutaway, and a vegetative nucleus precedes the sperm. Each sperm has a membrane cut edge and internal nucleus. Tube reveal and carrier positions are tied to the same path; the two sperm fates remain interchangeable. Embryo and endosperm ploidy are unchanged.
- **fungalHyphae:** uniformly transparent tubes became an open-front layered wall/inner membrane shell with exposed longitudinal cut edges and matching tip layers. Thickened septa have a rimmed, traversable central pore. The Spitzenkörper has a small-vesicle core and larger peripheral membrane carriers; moving carriers contain lumen/cargo detail. Apical synthases show subunits, and extracellular deposits are polymer bundles rather than isolated dots. Incoming trajectories converge at septal pores. Neurospora scope and reduced-supply control remain.

Validation: own Node smoke checks all four models, bilingual stages, finite geometry attributes and instance matrices, finite 2–20 unit bounds, distinct stages, stable node/geometry/material identities, repeated seeks, and all condition controls. Bundled the four modules with esbuild. No update creates geometry, materials, or scene nodes; repeating structures share buffers or use instancing.

Visual acceptance remains for root screenshot review. No model is certified against the accepted visual baseline from Node tests alone. Multiscale features and vesicle/protein dimensions are deliberately exaggerated for visibility; membrane recycling and full wall chemistry remain outside scope.
